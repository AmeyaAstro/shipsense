# Peak Season and Severe Weather

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Peak season
Peak season runs from **November 15 to January 10**. During peak season, carriers may take one
extra day beyond the scheduled days without penalty. Customers are still told the normal
scheduled days, but delivery credits start only from the second day late.

## Severe weather
When the Operations Control Center declares a severe-weather event for a region, delays in that
region during the event are **excused delays** and earn no delivery credit. The declaration must
name the region, start time and end time.

## Customer messaging during events
Post a banner on the order-tracking page within 2 hours of a declaration and email affected
customers with open orders within 1 business day.

## Staffing
During peak season, Customer Care hours extend to 7 days a week, 08:00 to 22:00 local time.
