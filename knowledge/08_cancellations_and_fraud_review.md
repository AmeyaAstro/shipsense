# Cancellations and Fraud Review

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Customer cancellations
Customers may cancel free of charge until the order ships. After shipping, the order must be
returned under the returns process; return shipping is free only if the order arrived late.

## Suspected fraud
Orders scored as suspected fraud are placed on a **review hold** and are not shipped. The Fraud
Review team must decide within 24 hours: release, or cancel and refund in full.

## Effect on delivery metrics
Canceled and suspected-fraud orders never ship, so they are **excluded** from on-time delivery
metrics and from the late-delivery model's training data. Reporting them as "on time" would
make performance look better than it is.

## Repeated cancellations
Accounts with more than 5 cancellations in 60 days are flagged for a manual account review.
