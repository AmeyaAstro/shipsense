# Corporate Account Service Level Agreement

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Who this applies to
Customers in the **Corporate** customer segment. Consumer and Home Office customers follow the
standard policies.

## Dedicated contact
Each Corporate account has a named Account Manager who must be copied on every Tier 2 and Tier 3
escalation for that account.

## Enhanced credits
Corporate accounts receive **double** the standard delivery-credit percentages (for example,
20% of the shipping charge for 1 day late instead of 10%), subject to the standard $250 cap
per order. Corporate credits are issued as account credit only, never as cash.

## Monthly reporting
Corporate accounts receive a monthly on-time delivery report by the 5th business day of the
following month, listing every late order and the credit applied.

## Upgrade eligibility
Corporate orders are eligible for courtesy upgrades regardless of order value.
