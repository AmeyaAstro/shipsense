# Damaged and Lost Shipments

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## When a shipment counts as lost
A shipment is declared **lost** when there has been no carrier scan for 10 business days after
its scheduled delivery date. Before that, it is treated as late, not lost.

## Customer remedy for lost shipments
The customer chooses either a free replacement shipped Same Day where available, or a full refund
of the order including shipping. Replacements do not count toward the customer's quarterly
courtesy-upgrade limit.

## Damaged on arrival
Customers must report damage within 7 days of delivery, with at least one photo. Damaged items
under $100 are refunded without a return; items of $100 or more must be returned for inspection.

## Carrier claims
Operations files a claim against the carrier within 30 days of the shipment being declared lost
or the damage being reported. Claims over $1,000 require the Carrier Management team's sign-off.
