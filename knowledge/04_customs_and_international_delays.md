# Customs and International Delays

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Scope
Applies to cross-border orders in the Africa, Europe, LATAM, Pacific Asia and USCA markets.

## Excused delays
A delay caused by a customs hold is an **excused delay**: no delivery credit is owed for the
days the shipment was held, provided the hold is documented by the carrier. Days late beyond
the documented hold still earn the normal credit.

## Required documents
Every international order must carry a commercial invoice and a harmonized-system (HS) code for
each item. Orders above $800 also need a certificate of origin. Missing documents are the most
common cause of holds and are **not** excused, because they are our responsibility.

## Market-specific notes
- **LATAM:** allow an extra 2 business days of customs processing for orders to Brazil and
  Argentina when quoting delivery dates to customers.
- **Europe:** shipments above EUR 150 may be charged import VAT at the border; the customer must
  be warned at checkout.
- **Pacific Asia:** holds longer than 5 business days must be escalated to the Trade Compliance team.

## Customer messaging
Tell the customer about a customs hold within 1 business day of the carrier reporting it, and
include the reason and the expected release date if known.
