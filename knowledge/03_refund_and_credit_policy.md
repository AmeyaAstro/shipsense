# Refund and Delivery Credit Policy

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Who qualifies
Any order delivered later than its scheduled days qualifies for a delivery credit, unless the
delay is an excused delay (customs holds, declared severe weather, or customer-caused delays).

## Credit amounts
- **1 day late:** 10% of the order's shipping charge as account credit.
- **2–3 days late:** 25% of the shipping charge.
- **4+ days late:** a full refund of the shipping charge, plus a $15 goodwill credit.
- **Same Day orders delivered late:** always a full shipping refund, regardless of how late.

Credits are capped at $250 per order. Amounts above the cap need Finance approval.

## Corporate accounts
Corporate-segment customers receive double the credit percentages above (see the Corporate
Account SLA), subject to the same $250 cap.

## How credits are issued
Credits are applied automatically within 5 business days of confirmed delivery. Cash refunds,
instead of credit, are available only on request and only for the Consumer and Home Office
segments. Refund requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.
