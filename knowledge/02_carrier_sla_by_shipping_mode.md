# Carrier Service Levels by Shipping Mode

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Scheduled delivery days
| Shipping mode | Scheduled days | Carrier on-time target |
|---|---|---|
| Same Day | 0 (delivered the day the order ships) | 98% |
| First Class | 1 | 95% |
| Second Class | 2 | 92% |
| Standard Class | 4 | 90% |

An order is **late** when actual shipping days exceed scheduled days. There is no grace period:
one day over is late.

## Carrier penalties
If a carrier misses its monthly on-time target for a mode, it pays a penalty of 3% of that
month's freight spend for the mode, rising to 6% if it misses the target by more than 10
percentage points. Penalties are reconciled quarterly by the Carrier Management team.

## Known weak spot
Historically, First Class rarely meets its 1-day schedule; most First Class orders take 2 days.
Sales staff must not promise next-day delivery on First Class without a Same Day upgrade.

## Cut-off times
Orders placed before 14:00 local warehouse time ship the same day. Orders placed after 14:00 are
treated as placed the next business day for scheduling purposes.
