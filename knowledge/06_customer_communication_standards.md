# Customer Communication Standards

> **FICTIONAL DOCUMENT.** Synthetic policy written for the ShipSense portfolio project. "ShipSense Logistics" is not a real company and none of this is real operational guidance.

## Response times
| Situation | Channel | Respond within |
|---|---|---|
| Order predicted late (high risk) | Email | 2 business hours |
| Order 1 day late | Email or SMS | 4 business hours |
| Order 4+ days late | Phone call | 1 business day |
| Customer complaint about a delay | Same channel the customer used | 24 hours |

## Tone and content
Messages must state the new expected delivery date, the reason for the delay if known, and any
credit the customer is entitled to. Apologise once; do not over-promise.

## Language
Messages are sent in the language of the customer's account. For LATAM orders, Spanish and
Portuguese templates are available; for Europe, English, French and German templates exist.

## What we never share
Never share carrier names of subcontractors, internal model scores, or other customers' data.
Never promise a delivery date that the carrier has not confirmed.
